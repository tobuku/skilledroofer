What Can You Build?

Woodworking isn't just a hobby - it adds real value to your property. Here are the most popular project categories homeowners tackle after handling their roof:

Storage & Organization

Garden sheds, tool storage cabinets, garage shelving, and workshop benches. If you're already cleaning up the yard for roofing access, building permanent storage is a natural next step.

Outdoor Living

Pergolas, deck furniture, Adirondack chairs, planter boxes, and picnic tables. Your new roof protects the house - now make the outdoor space match.

Home Improvements

Built-in bookshelves, mudroom benches, kitchen islands, and bathroom vanities. Interior woodworking projects increase home value and are satisfying to build yourself.

Workshop Essentials

Workbenches, tool chests, router tables, and clamp racks. Every serious DIYer needs a functional workshop - and it starts with the right bench.
